# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We are committed to a respectful and inclusive learning environment for everyone.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give feedback that focuses on code and ideas.
- Assume positive intent and communicate clearly.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, intimidation, or abusive language.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Reporting
Report conduct concerns to repository maintainers through private channels. Maintainers will review reports promptly and apply appropriate corrective action.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or limit participation when behavior violates this code.
